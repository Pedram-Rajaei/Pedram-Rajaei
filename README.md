### Hi, I'm Pedram

I'm a Ph.D. student in Biomedical Engineering at the **University of Houston** (Yousefi Lab). I build **probabilistic sequence models** (state-space models, latent SDEs, particle-filter inference) for noisy, small-sample **clinical and neural time series**, with a focus on calibrated uncertainty and interpretability.

- **Now:** latent SDE models for neural trajectories, and behavioral markers of suicide risk (DARPA FREUD program)
- **Before:** M.Sc. in Computer Science at Sharif University of Technology (interpretable ML for genomics), and 9 years as a software engineer
- **Looking for:** a Summer 2027 ML research internship
